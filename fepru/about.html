About us

Finance, audited privately.

01 — Who we are

Fepru is a financial and audit platform for institutions that cannot afford to send sensitive data outside their perimeter. We start with banks, microfinance institutions, corporate finance teams and the audit firms that serve them.

Our belief is simple: the intelligence should travel to your data, not the other way around. That turns the usual objection to AI in audit into the reason to adopt it.

02 — What we believe

Three principles guide everything we build.

01

Privacy first

No raw data leaves the customer. Trust boundaries stay exactly where they are today.

02

Explainable always

Auditors and regulators should be able to follow every conclusion back to its source.

03

Locally informed

Deep respect for SBP, SECP and FBR expectations, alongside IFRS and ISA standards.

03 — Where we’re going

We begin in Pakistan, then move to the wider region, including GCC and South Asian markets with strong data-sovereignty rules, and later to any regulated institution that values data residency.
